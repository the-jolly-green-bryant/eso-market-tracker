# TITLE

Tamriel Trade Index - ESO Pricing & Market Data

# SHORT

Accurate ESO pricing data across all megaservers. Look up item values, track trends, and power your add-ons with real
 market data.

# LONG

Tamriel Trade Index is a fast, reliable source of pricing data for The Elder Scrolls Online, built for players, addon
 developers, and data nerds alike.

Get access to real market values across all megaservers, with clean, structured data you can actually use.

What you get
- Up-to-date pricing data for ESO items
- Coverage across all platforms and regions
- Simple, predictable data format
- Designed for addon integration and external tools
- Built for builders

Whether you're creating an addon, a website, or your own trading tools, this API is meant to be easy to work with. No
 weird formatting, no unnecessary layers, just the data you need.

Why it exists

Most pricing tools are either locked down, inconsistent, or hard to integrate with. This is a clean alternative built to
 be open, usable, and dependable.

Use it your way
- Build custom trading tools
- Power your own addons
- Analyze market trends
- Or just look up what your items are worth
